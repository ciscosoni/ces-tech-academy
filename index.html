Courses for network engineers

Learn it the way it happens on a project.

Every lesson opens with a real-world case. You investigate it, learn what is behind it, and then fix it yourself in a simulator. No slides are read to you.

How a lesson works

  1. A caseAn outage, a design workshop, an audit finding. Something is wrong or undecided.
  2. You investigateThe narrator stops and hands over. Pick a check and see what you find.
  3. The idea, drawnDiagrams build up in step with the explanation, one idea at a time.
  4. You fix itA simulator lets you change the design, send traffic and prove the fix.
  5. LabThen the same thinking on real equipment or a vendor sandbox.
